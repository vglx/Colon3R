In-vivo reconstruction

Qualitative reconstructions from monocular RGB sequences. No geometric ground truth is available for these in-vivo examples.

Overview

Colon3R adapts VGGT from labeled phantom and simulated data to unlabeled in-vivo colonoscopy. Hierarchical quasi-rigid reliability selects cross-view supervision at the sequence, directed-pair and pixel levels. Continued source supervision and constrained target updates preserve the coupled camera, depth and pointmap geometry during adaptation.
